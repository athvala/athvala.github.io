2019 —

Eagle Events Operator-run

Started it in high school. Now Slovenia's largest catering provider, up 200% last year. In 2025 we took beverage operations at Pivo in Cvetje — 60,000 people, the country's biggest festival — from a supplier who had held it for twenty years.

New EU rules had just banned single-use cups, and everyone was treating the reusable cup as one problem: a deposit item you hand back at the bar. That was what created the queues. We made the cup a souvenir people were meant to keep, moved returns off the bars entirely, and paid a bounty on volume — so attendees collected the cups off the ground themselves. Operator-run since August 2025.

Sense Club Closed at a loss

Opened a nightclub in Celje and closed it twelve months later at a personal loss. The failure was structural, not operational: I signed a lease and took on fixed monthly costs sized for a demand curve I had only ever seen in events, where a bad weekend costs nothing because you simply don't run the event. Fixed costs don't give you that option.
